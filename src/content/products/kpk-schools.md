KPK Schools is our first product, built and run by KPK Trinex Technologies. Schools use it every day to admit students,
take attendance, collect fees, run exams, pay their staff and keep their accounts - with every printed document in
English and Hindi.

Their data is stored in India (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai), encrypted and backed up daily; each school's data is kept
apart, access goes by role, and every change is kept in a history.

A demo is free, and the price is quoted for each school by its size and needs.
